Privacy Policy

Barcode & Price Scanner (Android app, package com.geekapps) · Developer: Geek-Troubleshoot.com · Last updated: 13 November 2026

In short: the app reads barcodes on your phone. It does not have accounts, does not upload your camera images or photos, and does not sell your data. The free version shows ads from Google AdMob, which collects some device data to show and measure ads, as described below.

1. Information the app processes on your device

  • Camera. The camera is used only while the Scan screen is open, to detect barcodes and QR codes. Video frames are processed on your device and are not recorded, stored or sent anywhere.
  • Photos you choose. If you use "Scan from photo", only the single image you pick is read on your device to find a barcode. It is not uploaded or kept by the app.
  • Scan results. The text or number inside a code is shown to you on screen. The app does not keep a scan history and does not send scan results to us.
  • Settings. Your price-label settings (currency, label format, price or weight) are saved only on your device.
  • Codes you create. Text you type on the Create screen is used only to draw the code on your device. If you choose to share the image, it goes only to the app you pick.

2. Actions you start

Some results offer buttons such as Open link, Search product, Call, Email, SMS, Maps or Wi-Fi settings. These open other apps or your web browser only when you tap them. What happens next is governed by the privacy policy of that app or website. The app shows a warning for links that look unsafe, but it cannot guarantee a website is safe.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows banner and full-screen ads provided by Google AdMob. To show, limit and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect and process:

  • Your device's advertising ID and app-set ID
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it
  • Device and app information (model, OS version, language, app version)
  • Ad interactions, such as ad views and taps, and diagnostic data

This data is collected directly by Google and handled under Google's Privacy Policy. You can learn how Google uses data from apps that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Your choices: where required by law (for example in the EEA, UK and Switzerland) the app asks for your consent before showing personalised ads. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalisation, in your Android settings under Google → Ads or Privacy → Ads.

4. Data sharing and sale

We do not sell personal data. We do not receive or store your scans, photos or personal information on any server. The only third party that receives data from the app is Google, for advertising as described above.

5. Permissions

  • Camera – to scan codes.
  • Photos (via Android's photo picker) – only the image you choose.
  • Internet / network state – to load ads.
  • Advertising ID – used by Google AdMob for ads.

6. Children

The app is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Security and retention

Because scan data, photos and settings stay on your device, they are removed when you clear the app's data or uninstall it. Data collected by Google for ads is retained under Google's policies.

8. Changes

We may update this policy when the app changes. The new version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
